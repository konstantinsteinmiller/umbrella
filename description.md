# Elemental Barrage — Store Copy

## Short Description (max 150 chars)

Pull back, release, and watch elemental balls ricochet through marching monsters. Merge balls, topple bosses, reach the deepest row!

## Long Description (max 500 chars)

Elemental Barrage is a casual volley shooter. Monsters march down the board row by row — drag back, aim and release a barrage of elemental balls that bounce through them. Grab +1 pickups: when your ball tray overflows, twin balls merge into mightier elementals with burn, freeze, chain lightning and more. Every 10 rows a boss enrages; beat it to set a checkpoint. Buy the glowing upgrade, collect launchers and ball skins, and chase the Daily Barrage.

## Controls

**Touch & mouse only — no keyboard needed.**

- **Drag back & release** — pull back anywhere on the board like a slingshot; the dotted line shows where the first bounce lands. Release to fire the whole volley.
- **Recall** — ends a slow volley instantly (long volleys also fast-forward on their own).
- **Tap the glowing upgrade** — the cheapest upgrade you can afford always glows.

## How to Play

1. **Aim and release.** Your volley streams out and ricochets off walls and monsters. Every hit deals damage; the launcher moves to where your first ball lands.
2. **Stop the march.** After each turn every monster steps one row closer. If one reaches your launcher line, it's a breach.
3. **Collect +1 balls.** Fly through the +1 bubbles to grow your volley.
4. **Merge by overflowing.** When your tray is full, the next pickup merges the lowest twin pair into the next elemental — each tier hits harder and gains a power: burn, knockback, pierce, freeze, splash, crush, chain lightning, poison, Midas gold and eruptions.
5. **Pop skill bubbles.** Meteor, Warcry, Legion Call, Soul Pact, Shield Crest, Executioner's Mark, Heal and Revive trigger when a ball hits them.
6. **Beat the bosses.** Every 10 rows a boss marches in and enrages at 30% health. Defeat it to set a checkpoint and open its chest.
7. **Never a game over.** A breach offers a Meteor Storm continue, or a restart from your last boss checkpoint — you keep every coin.
8. **Upgrade and collect.** Ball Damage, Tray Capacity, Launch Speed, Pickup Luck and Coin Bounty; unlock 50 elemental launchers and 12 elemental ball skins, and play the seeded Daily Barrage.
