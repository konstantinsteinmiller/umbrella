# Umbrella

## Short description

Open your umbrella, ride the wind and float to the finish! A one-button sky platformer with 27 handcrafted levels.

## Description

Help Pweeg the postman cross the rooftops with nothing but his trusty umbrella. Open it to catch rising winds and drift gently down, close it to drop fast, and time it right to dodge spiky mines and grumpy Walldorf chimney creatures. Collect stars, beat the clock for three-star ratings and conquer 27 handcrafted levels in a cozy, hand-painted world, then build your own levels and play what the community made.

## How to play

1. Walk Pweeg to the edge of a platform and step off.
2. Open the umbrella to float: rising winds carry you up, and you drift slowly down.
3. Close the umbrella to fall fast. Don't fall too far onto hard ground, or you splat!
4. Avoid mines, spikes and the Walldorfs. Touching them sends you back to the last checkpoint.
5. Open the umbrella at high speed and it flips inside out, so brake early.
6. Land on the finish platform to clear the level. On timed levels, finish fast for three stars.

## Controls

**Keyboard**

- ← / → or A / D: walk
- Space or Enter: open / close the umbrella
- R: restart the level
- Esc: pause

**Touch**

- Left stick: walk
- Umbrella button: open / close the umbrella
