# Merge Idle War — Store Copy

## Short Description (max 150 chars)

Merge elemental units into a 10-tier army and crush endless enemy waves. Combos, fever gold rushes, meteor storms — the war never stops!

## Long Description (max 500 chars)

Merge Idle War is a hybrid-casual merge auto-battler. Tap to spawn recruits, drag matching units together to forge ever-stronger elemental fighters — from Living Droplet to the black-hole-wielding Cosmic Mass — and drop them into the arena, where they battle endless waves on their own. Chain kills into combos, ignite FEVER for double gold, unleash the Meteor Storm ultimate and buy permanent upgrades. Bosses enrage, the gate cracks, but the war never ends — how far can you push?

## Controls

**Touch & mouse only — no keyboard needed.**

- **Tap SPAWN** — fills the spawn meter; every 4 taps a Tier-1 recruit pops onto the merge grid.
- **Drag & drop on the grid** — drag a unit onto an identical one to merge them into the next tier, or onto an empty slot to move it.
- **Drag into the arena** — pull a unit from the grid up into the battlefield to deploy it as a fighter (max 7 on the field).
- **Drop onto a same-tier fighter** — field-merge: the fighter tiers up in place and is fully healed.
- **Tap the Ultimate button** — when fully charged, unleashes the Meteor Storm.
- **Tap the upgrade buttons** — spend gold on War Horns, Vitality, Bounty and the Imp Sergeant.

## How to Play

1. **Spawn recruits.** Tap the SPAWN button to fill its meter — every full meter drops a Tier-1 Living Droplet onto the 4×4 grid.
2. **Merge to power up.** Drag two identical units together to create one unit of the next tier. Ten tiers await, each roughly twice as strong, each with its own ability — burn, poison, chain lightning, freezing novas and more.
3. **Deploy your army.** Drag units from the grid into the arena. Fighters battle automatically, marching against endless enemy waves that attack your Lab Gate from the right.
4. **Field-merge to heal.** Dropping a grid unit onto a deployed fighter of the same tier upgrades it on the spot at full health — your best mid-battle rescue.
5. **Chain kills.** Rapid kills build a combo that multiplies gold. Reach combo ×10 to ignite FEVER: 8 seconds of faster attacks and double gold.
6. **Unleash the Ultimate.** Kills charge the meteor meter — tap it when it pulses to rain seven meteors on the enemy horde.
7. **Upgrade forever.** Spend gold on permanent attack, health and bounty boosts, and unlock the Imp Sergeant to merge and deploy for you while you watch the carnage.
8. **Survive the bosses.** Every 5th wave a named boss attacks — and enrages below 30% health. If the gate falls, the wave simply restarts: fallen fighters stay dead, but the war is never over. Beat your best wave!
