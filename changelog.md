Umbrella — changelog (newest first)

== Revision 16 — 2026-11-04 ==
- [x] Landscape lock: a phone or tablet held upright is covered by a full-screen "turn your phone sideways" card (the game freezes behind it like behind any menu and resumes the moment the device is turned; a desktop window is never locked, however narrow). A phone turns onto its side with an overshoot while an arrow sweeps round and the world drawn inside it goes upright; the drawn build gets the flat marker-outlined version, the lantern build lit washi, lacquer, gold leaf, hanging lanterns, stars and a moon, the clay build plasticine under falling rain. Reduced motion keeps the phone turned and only fades. Copy in all 21 languages (`umbrella.rotate.*`)
- [x] The pre-game intro cutscene is switched off by a feature flag: `VITE_APP_INTRO_ENABLED=false` in the root .env (a new player goes straight to Level 1, and the pause menu's "watch the intro" button is hidden). Set it to `true` or delete the line to bring the cutscene back; nothing of the cutscene was removed. The "click to play" screen (it only served to give the page focus before the intro began by itself) is skipped with it: the game is live at once, and sound unlocks on the first touch or key; with the flag on it comes back
- [x] Level editor: the standalone arrow (tool Arrow, key A): the hint board's big black marker arrow as an item of its own — as many as a level needs; drag it to move, the blue dot ahead of its tip turns it (15° steps while snapping), the green corner resizes it, and the properties give four direction buttons, an angle box and a size slider. Same look as the board's arrow in every build (`arrows` in the level)
- [x] Level editor, designer only: the picture board (tool Picture board, key I): an image holder in the look of the hint board, for the instruction images of the first levels. Drop an image file onto the canvas to make a board from it, onto a board (or its properties' drop field, or click it to choose) to fill or replace it; "Fit board to image", "Remove image", and a switch for the paper board around it. The image is shrunk to a small WebP, named by a hash of its bytes, and stored on the levels bucket Worker (`POST /images`, `GET /images/:id`) while the level keeps only the name (`pictures` in the level); without the editor password it is kept in the browser and uploads on the next sync. `pnpm levels:pull` (run before every build) bakes the campaign's images into public/level-images/ so portal builds need no request. Deploy the Worker (`wrangler deploy` in workers/levels-bucket) before the first upload
- [x] Camera: a level that shows its whole world on a landscape screen is now framed on what is in it (the start, the finish, platforms, hazards, collectibles, boards; currents only a short way past them) with a margin, never smaller than the whole-world fit and at most 1.6x closer, so the start and the finish are in view from the first frame, with little empty space at the sides and none below the out-of-bounds line (the view never goes below the world's bottom; a screen taller than the world sits on its bottom, the spare space goes above). Scrolling, open-top, fixed-screen and portrait levels keep their cameras
- [x] Picture boards (the editor's image holders) hang: two ropes come down from behind the top edge of the screen to knots on the board's top corners, and a light wind swings the board a few units back and forth with a hair of twist (slow gusts of different periods, each board on its own phase); the ropes are redrawn every frame from their fixed anchors to the knots, so they follow the board. Flat cord on the drawn build, gold-and-lacquer cord on the lantern and clay builds. The sway is off with reduced motion. Picture-board images load by fetch and createImageBitmap (not an <img>), because the Pages build's CSP allows no blob: or bucket host in img-src; the bucket is tried before the baked copy
